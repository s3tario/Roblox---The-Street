THE STREET

From campus recruiting to a Wall Street career

A career-first Roblox experience where players build a profile, win an
internship, trade fictional markets, survive performance reviews, earn
promotions and choose their finance exit path.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  55-65 HRS        Target: a meaningful level-100 journey in about 55-65
                   hours of active play. Players keep purchased cosmetics
                   and achievements through career setbacks.

01 / DESIGN SECTION

Product vision and session loop

The fantasy is not “click to earn.” It is building a career under
pressure.

One-sentence pitch

Start as a college student with a randomly generated background, recruit
into investment banking, make decisions in a fictional stock market,
rise through promotions, and branch into a specialist finance career.
Great performance opens doors faster; repeated poor decisions can lead
to probation or being fired.

Core play session

  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Step        What the player does            Reward / consequence
  ----------- ------------------------------- -----------------------------
  1. Read the Review fictional market news,   Forms a hypothesis before
  tape        earnings and daily catalysts.   trading.

  2. Do the   Complete a short valuation,     Career XP, work score, salary
  job         pitchbook, research or deal     and reputation.
              minigame.                       

  3. Make     Buy, hold or sell fictional     Risk-adjusted trading score
  decisions   shares; manage exposure and     and virtual gains/losses.
              risk.                           

  4. Build a  Shop outfits, customize desk /  Visible identity and social
  life        apartment and network with      roleplay.
              other players.                  

  5. Get      Receive a weekly performance    Promotion, coaching,
  reviewed    review and promotion            probation or termination.
              eligibility.                    
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------

Four product pillars

-   Career fantasy: each promotion changes the work, office access, deal
    scale and social status.

-   Investment gameplay: information, uncertainty and risk management
    matter more than clicking speed.

-   Personal identity: wardrobe, office design, profile card and career
    history are persistent reasons to return.

-   Comeback stories: firing is a recoverable setback that changes the
    career narrative, not a deletion of the player’s whole account.

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Design rule                                                           |
|                                                                       |
| Every 10-15 minutes, players should make a decision, earn a visible   |
| reward, unlock a new task or receive useful feedback. Do not stretch  |
| a 60-hour journey with repetitive timers.                             |

Make the profile roll exciting, readable and monetizable without letting
it determine the whole career.

                          avoiding preventable       improve with routines /

                                                     uncertainty.

                          and informational          play and events.
                          interviews.

Reroll design

-   At setup: generate three candidate backgrounds and include one free
    reroll. Let the player compare the full profile before locking in.

-   Earnable option: a Profile Refresh token from a campus challenge,
    seasonal goal or career milestone. Never make a single bad roll a
    permanent trap.

-   Paid option A - Profile Reroll: 49 R$ as a test price. Since this is
    a Robux-funded random outcome, disclose every possible outcome and
    numerical odds, check Roblox PolicyService eligibility, and restrict
    purchase where required [4].

-   Paid option B - Background Rebuild: 99 R$ test price. A
    deterministic editor lets the player select from eligible school /
    region / major / family archetypes. This is easier to understand
    than paid randomization.

Career path and 55-65 hour level curve

Use level 100 as the cap. Early promotions arrive quickly; later
promotions require skill and larger projects.

  range                     cumulative

                                         mock stock pitch, networking and

                                         notes, data cleanup and initial
                                         market portfolio.

  16-30    IB Analyst       7-17 hours   Valuation models, comparable
                                         companies, DCF, earnings
                                         reactions and live deal teams.

           Managing                      and qualify for exit recruiting.
           Director

           career                        Estate or Corporate Development;
                                         master a distinct mechanic.

           Portfolio Leader              or assets, complete signature
           / Family Office               projects and build a firm.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

Promotion should be earned in multiple ways

-   Standard route: complete core assignments, meet reputation
    requirements and pass a skills evaluation.

-   Fast-track route: sustained risk-adjusted market performance,
    excellent work quality and completed signature deals reduce
    assignment requirements by up to 20%.

-   Alternative route: weaker market results can be offset by excellent
    deal work and risk discipline; this prevents the game becoming a
    pure stock-price lottery.

-   Career exits: after IB leadership, pick a specialist track.
    Alternate career records preserve previous achievements, while the
    selected path determines the current progression journey.

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Endgame content                                                       |
|                                                                       |
| Level 100 should unlock more difficult contracts, firm customization, |
| legacy goals and seasonal challenges rather than ending the game. The |
| 55-65 hours is the core career journey, not the lifespan of the       |
| experience.                                                           |

05 / DESIGN SECTION

Performance reviews, firing and The Wipeout

Bad investing can create consequences, but a single unlucky market swing
should not erase a career.

Suggested review score

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Review component     Weight   Server-calculated evidence
  -------------------- -------- ------------------------------------------
  Risk-adjusted        35%      Excess returns vs. fictional benchmark,
  investment                    drawdown and volatility over a review
  performance                   window.

  Risk management      25%      Position limits, leverage rules,
                                concentration and response to warnings.

  Work quality         25%      Model accuracy, deal assignment quality,
                                research tasks and deadline reliability.

  Team / client        15%      Deal participation, networking events,
  reputation                    mentoring and client-pitch quality.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Score      Status            Outcome
  ---------- ----------------- --------------------------------------------
  85-100     Star performer    Eligible for fast-track promotion, premier
                               deal assignments and larger earned bonuses.

  70-84      On track          Standard advancement.

  55-69      Underperforming   Coaching tasks and no immediate promotion.

  40-54      Probation         Risk limits tighten; improvement plan; next
                               review is high stakes.

  Below 40   Critical / fired  Current role ends; employment recovery
  twice                        options become available.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Wipeout rules

-   Trigger dismissal after two critical reviews, or after a major
    repeated risk-limit violation that the player ignored after
    warnings.

-   Consequences may remove current job status, active deal pipeline, a
    portion of current career XP and virtual trading capital. Explain
    the exact loss before the final confirmation.

-   Never remove Robux-purchased outfits, permanent cosmetics, badges,
    earned career history or account-level achievements.

Recovery routes

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Route            Cost            Effect
  ---------------- --------------- ---------------------------------------
  Re-entry         Free            15-25 minute recovery sequence, rejoin
  interview                        one rank lower with modest capital.

  Career           Suggested       Restore former title and rank
  Reinstatement    99-149 R$       eligibility; still complete probation
                                   and rebuild the portfolio.

  Capital          Suggested 49-99 Restore a capped portion of lost career
  Reconstruction   R$              cash. Exclude paid/restored capital
                                   from competitive scores.

  Fresh start      Free            Return to student / intern; preserve
                                   permanent cosmetics and career history.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Avoid pay-to-not-lose pressure                                        |
|                                                                       |
| Show all recovery options in a neutral career-services screen. No     |
| automatic purchase pop-up on firing, no countdown pressure and no     |
| purchase required to continue playing.                                |

06 / DESIGN SECTION

Fictional market, deals and specialist realms

Every division needs a distinct loop, not just a new office backdrop.

Fictional market model

-   Use made-up companies and sectors: Apex AI, Meridian Bank, Volt
    Automotive, Summit Properties and Titan Energy. No actual money or
    real-stock integration.

-   Prices move from disclosed fundamentals, scheduled earnings, broad
    market events and limited uncertainty. Players analyze information
    rather than guess an entirely opaque random chart.

-   Track cash, invested value, realized P/L, unrealized P/L, drawdown,
    benchmark return and risk-adjusted score separately.

-   Risk controls: concentration limits, warnings, optional stop orders,
    and review windows long enough that one random move does not decide
    employment.

Lifestyle progression

Players earn ordinary clothing, watches, accessories, desk items and
apartment upgrades with in-game salary cash. Robux cosmetics add new
styles and customization options, not career score or stock-market
intelligence.

Robux-to-value rules

-   All products explain exact contents before purchase. Use Roblox
    Developer Products for repeatable items and Passes for one-time
    entitlements [5].

-   No Robux purchase increases stock returns, gives an exclusive source
    of market information, or directly improves risk-adjusted
    leaderboard scores.

-   Every intentional resettable/lost gameplay resource gets a free
    earn-back route and a clearly defined optional recovery purchase,
    where appropriate.

-   Bug-related losses are restored through support at no charge. Paid
    recovery should never replace good save reliability.

Implementation plan

-   Use server-side score calculation and OrderedDataStore / persistent
    global ranking infrastructure. Roblox documents persistent Global
    rankings and OrderedDataStores; availability and built-in
    leaderboard constraints should be checked against the current
    Creator Hub setup [1][2].

-   Build custom in-world boards for multiple categories, since Roblox’s
    built-in PlayerList global view has constraints and may support only
    one registered active persistent leaderboard per experience at a
    time [1].

-   Refresh display boards on a controlled interval; cache reads; do not
    call global data services every frame.

-   Add seasonal, friends-only and regional filters in custom UI where
    data volume and moderation permit.

Abuse controls

-   The server validates purchases, inventory changes, trade actions,
    score updates and review outcomes. The client never tells the server
    its own “final score.”

-   Flag impossible returns, implausible trade volume, teleport / timer
    exploits and repeated disconnect abuse. Keep a human review and
    appeal path for leaderboard bans.

09 / DESIGN SECTION

Technical architecture and persistence

The economy is only as trustworthy as its save and transaction systems.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  System         Server-owned data         Implementation notes
  -------------- ------------------------- -------------------------------
  Player profile Background roll, school   Store in standard DataStore;
                 tier, GPA, skill          version schema and validate
                 attributes, current role. every field [3].

  Career state   Level, reputation,        Only server-authoritative code
                 promotion history,        updates progression.
                 probation status,         
                 specialist path.          

  Portfolio      Cash, positions,          Server controls prices and
  ledger         transaction history, P/L, fills; use transaction IDs to
                 benchmark series.         avoid duplicate trades.

  Inventory /    Cosmetics, outfit         Grant after verified receipt;
  entitlements   bundles, pass ownership   make purchase processing
                 and recovery purchases.   idempotent [5].

  Leaderboard    Career prestige,          Write numeric values to ordered
  score          normalized returns, deal  data stores from server code
                 score, season ID.         [1][2].

  Audit /        Review history, wipeout   Keep enough history to resolve
  support        reason, restore action    disputed purchases or lost
                 and product receipt.      progress.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

Build safeguards

-   Use server-authoritative market clocks and price updates. Do not let
    local client time determine returns or review deadlines.

-   Design an explicit state machine: Active -> Review Due -> Coaching /
    Probation -> Fired -> Re-entry / Reinstated.

-   Apply rate limits and input validation to remotes; never trust
    client-supplied price, currency amount or leaderboard value.

-   Autosave at sensible checkpoints, save on leave and use
    recovery-safe data writes. Roblox DataStores persist data between
    sessions and server-side calls protect the data boundary [3].

-   Test duplicate purchase receipts, server shutdowns during saves,
    invalid values, rejoining mid-trade and old schema migrations before
    launch.

    assignment and first promotion conversion.

Open design questions to validate in playtests

-   Should family background meaningfully affect starting cash, or
    should it only affect contacts and expenses? Prefer a low-impact
    cash effect.

-   Is a 55-65 hour target still fun for players who only want market /
    fashion gameplay? Provide alternate goals that do not require level
    100.

-   Does paid reinstatement feel like a convenience or a penalty toll?
    If testers describe it as a toll, lower the wipeout punishment
    instead of pushing purchase conversion.

-   Which specialized finance path gets the highest organic selection
    and repeat play? Use that to choose the first expansion.
